# OZ Fashion House shop upgrade

## What will change
- Replace the showcase-only experience with a real product catalogue for Bride, Groom, Bridesmaids, and Accessories.
- Add category filters, product details, prices, quantity controls, and a shopping bag.
- Let customers send their complete order directly to OZ Fashion House through WhatsApp.
- Rework the header so the logo and navigation are clear on every screen.
- Add dedicated About Us, Contact, and Journal pages with useful business information and visible links from the main menu.
- Enrich the homepage with featured products, stronger editorial composition, and clearer shopping calls to action.

## Technical details
- Keep the catalogue frontend-only and pre-filled; WhatsApp remains the checkout channel.
- Create separate `/collections`, `/about`, `/contact`, and `/journal` pages with unique page metadata.
- Move the professional header, footer, and shopping bag into a shared site layout across all pages.
- Share catalogue data and cart state across the site, with a responsive slide-out shopping bag.
- Preserve the approved black, champagne-gold, and ivory identity while increasing contrast and visual hierarchy.
- Verify desktop and mobile browsing, filtering, bag updates, and WhatsApp order formatting.
